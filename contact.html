FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How fast can you get to me?

Our average arrival time is about 30 minutes, depending on your location, traffic and weather. We'll give you an estimated time when you call.

How much does a tow cost?

Pricing depends on vehicle type, distance and service. We always give you a clear quote over the phone before we dispatch — no hidden fees.

Do you work with insurance and roadside plans?

Yes. We work with most insurance providers and roadside assistance programs. Have your policy details ready when you call.

Can you tow motorcycles and large trucks?

Absolutely. Our fleet includes flatbeds for motorcycles and specialty vehicles, plus heavy-duty wreckers for commercial trucks, buses and RVs.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, all major credit and debit cards, and mobile payments.
