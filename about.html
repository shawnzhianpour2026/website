Our story

Driven by a simple idea: be there when it matters

Shawline Truck started with one truck and a promise — that no driver should be left waiting alone on the side of the road. Today we run a growing fleet of flatbeds, wreckers and service vehicles, but that promise hasn't changed.

Our dispatchers answer every call personally, our operators are trained and certified, and every vehicle we touch is handled as if it were our own. Whether it's a midnight breakdown, an accident recovery or a planned vehicle transport, we show up ready.
